# Selling on UdyogConnect

## Registering a business
Register as a business owner and submit your business details: name, category, location, description, contact details, opening hours and documents. Your business is pending until an admin reviews it. An admin can approve it, reject it with a reason, or ask you to make changes and resubmit. Customers can only see your business after it is approved.

## Suspended businesses
An admin can suspend a business. A suspended business is hidden from customers until the suspension is lifted. Contact support if your business is suspended.

## Managing your business
From the business dashboard you can edit your profile and logo, set opening days and hours, switch the shop open or closed, choose delivery and delivery radius, add products (price, discount, stock, images) and services (price, duration, time slots), handle orders and bookings, reply to customer messages and see your reviews and analytics.

## Orders as a seller
Accept or reject new orders, mark them as preparing, then send them out for delivery. Ask the customer for the 6-digit delivery OTP at the door to complete the order. Rejecting an order returns its stock.

## eSewa for sellers
Connect your own eSewa merchant account from the business dashboard to accept eSewa payments. Your secret key is encrypted on the server and never shown in the browser.

## Data privacy for sellers
Each seller can only see their own business, products, services, orders, bookings, reviews and analytics. The AI assistant follows the same rule.
