# Payments

## Payment methods
UdyogConnect supports Cash on Delivery (COD) and eSewa. eSewa is available only for businesses that have connected their own eSewa merchant account; other businesses accept Cash on Delivery.

## Paying with eSewa
When you pay with eSewa you are sent to eSewa to complete the payment. The order is created only after UdyogConnect verifies the payment directly with eSewa, so an order is never marked paid without eSewa's confirmation. If the payment fails or is cancelled, no order is placed and you can try again.

## Cash on Delivery
With Cash on Delivery you pay the business when the order arrives. The order is marked paid when delivery is confirmed with the delivery OTP or with "Order Received".

## Payment security
Merchant secret keys are stored encrypted on the server and are never sent to the browser. UdyogConnect never asks for your eSewa password or PIN in chat.
