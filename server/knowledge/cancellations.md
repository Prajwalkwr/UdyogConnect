# Cancellations

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel your own order from your customer dashboard while it is still placed or accepted. Once the business has started preparing it, or it is out for delivery, the order can no longer be cancelled. A business can reject an order that it has not yet accepted.

## Cancelling a booking
Customers can cancel their own service bookings from the dashboard. Only the business can confirm, complete or reject a booking.

## Refunds
UdyogConnect does not describe an automatic refund process for cancelled orders. For an eSewa payment on a cancelled or rejected order, contact the business through chat.
