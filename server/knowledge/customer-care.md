# Customer care

## Customer care contact
Ask the assistant for "customer care" to see UdyogConnect's current support contact details. UdyogConnect support never asks for your password, delivery OTP or eSewa PIN.

## Contact a business or seller
Open the business profile and use the message button to chat with the business, or call or email them using the contact details shown on their profile. You can also ask the assistant for a business's phone number by its name.

## Problems with an order
First check the order status in your customer dashboard under Orders. For questions about the items or the delivery time, message the business. If the business does not reply or the problem is not solved, contact UdyogConnect customer care with your order details.

## Payment problems
An eSewa order is only created after eSewa confirms the payment. If money left your eSewa account but no order appears, contact UdyogConnect customer care with the payment time and amount.

## Reporting a business or a scam
To report a business, a fake listing or a scam, contact UdyogConnect customer care. Admins review reports and can suspend businesses that break the rules.

## Account and sign-in problems
If you forgot your password, use "Forgot Password?" on the login screen. If you still cannot sign in, contact UdyogConnect customer care from the email address on your account.

## Notifications
The bell icon at the top of the page shows updates about your orders and bookings, such as when an order is accepted or a booking is confirmed.
