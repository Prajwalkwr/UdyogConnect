# Ordering, checkout and delivery

## How ordering works
Add products to your cart from a business profile or product page, then open the cart and check out. If your cart has items from several businesses, UdyogConnect creates a separate order for each business. You need to be signed in as a customer to place an order.

## Prices and totals
The official price, discount, delivery fee and total of every order are calculated by the UdyogConnect server from the business's current listings when you check out. Prices shown in your browser cannot change the official bill.

## Delivery address
For delivery you enter your full name, a 10-digit Nepali mobile number starting with 97 or 98, your city and a street or landmark. Saved delivery addresses in your dashboard fill checkout in automatically. You can choose pickup instead.

## Order status
An order moves through these steps: placed, accepted, preparing, out for delivery (dispatched), and completed. A business can also reject a newly placed order, and the stock is returned. You can follow each step in your customer dashboard under Orders, and you get a notification when the status changes.

## Delivery OTP
When a business sends your order out for delivery you receive a 6-digit delivery code (OTP). Share it with the delivery person only when you have received your order. You can also confirm delivery yourself with the "Order Received" button. After 5 wrong OTP attempts the code is locked and the order must be confirmed with "Order Received".

## Bills
A bill is generated from the official order data when your order is placed (for eSewa, once the payment is confirmed) and emailed as a PDF to the address on the order. You can view, download or resend the bill from the order details.
