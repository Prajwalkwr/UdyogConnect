# About UdyogConnect

## What UdyogConnect is
UdyogConnect is a local business marketplace for Nepal. Customers discover nearby shops, restaurants and service providers, order products, book services, chat with businesses and leave reviews. Business owners (sellers) list their business, products and services and manage orders and bookings from the business dashboard. Admins review and approve businesses and moderate the platform.

## Account types
There are three kinds of accounts. Customers browse, order, book and review. Sellers own one or more businesses and manage them from the business dashboard. Admins approve businesses, handle reports and see marketplace-wide statistics. Each account only sees its own private information.

## Which businesses are shown
Only businesses that an admin has approved are shown to customers, appear in search and receive recommendations. Businesses that are pending approval, rejected, asked to make changes, or suspended are hidden from customers.

## Products and services
A business can offer products, services, or both. Products are bought through the cart and checkout. Services are booked for a date and time slot from the business profile. Product prices, discounts and stock come directly from the business's current listings.

## Open and closed status
A business is shown as open or closed using its opening days and opening hours, in Nepal time. A seller can also switch the shop open or closed manually; the manual switch lasts until the next scheduled opening or closing time, then the normal hours apply again.

## Delivery
Each business chooses whether it offers delivery and how far it delivers (5 km by default). Customers can also choose pickup at checkout.

## Recommendations
The home page recommends approved businesses using your location, what you order, book, save, review and view, and what is popular nearby this week. You can reset your recommendation history from the "How it works?" panel in the recommendations section. Orders, bookings and saved businesses stay in your account.
