# Reviews and ratings

## Writing a review
Signed-in users can review a business, product or service with a rating from 1 to 5 stars and a comment of at least 5 characters, optionally with a photo. Business owners cannot review their own business or its products. You can edit or delete your own reviews.

## How ratings are calculated
A business's rating is the average of its business reviews, and the review count is the number of those reviews. A business with no reviews has no rating yet; UdyogConnect never shows a made-up rating.

## Reporting a review
Reviews and businesses can be reported for an admin to check. Admins can resolve or dismiss reports.
