# Help and frequently asked questions

## Forgot password
On the login screen choose "Forgot Password?" and enter your email. If an account exists, a reset link is emailed to you. The link works once and expires after 30 minutes. After resetting, sign in with your new password; other signed-in sessions are signed out.

## Chatting with a business
Open a business profile and use the message button to chat with the business. Sellers reply from the business dashboard. You can see whether the business is online.

## Saving businesses
Use the heart icon on a business card to save it to your wishlist. Saved businesses appear in your customer dashboard and help personalise recommendations.

## Finding businesses near you
Use "Near me" on the home page or allow location access in the AI assistant. Distances are calculated from your location to each business's map location. Without your location, distances are estimated from the area you type, your saved address, or Kathmandu.

## About the AI assistant
The UdyogConnect AI assistant searches live UdyogConnect listings and these help pages before answering. It only recommends approved businesses and shows the actual listings it used. Always check the business or product page for the final price, stock and opening hours. The assistant never asks for your password, OTP or payment PIN.
