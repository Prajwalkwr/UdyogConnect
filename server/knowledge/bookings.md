# Booking services

## How booking works
Open the business profile, go to the Services tab, choose a service, then pick a date and an available time slot. A booking starts as pending until the business confirms it. Booking status can be pending, confirmed, completed, cancelled or rejected.

## Booking limits
Each business sets how much notice it needs before a booking (30 minutes by default), how far ahead you can book (60 days by default) and the length of its time slots. Slots that are already taken, outside opening hours or on the business's closed days cannot be booked.

## Home service
Some services are marked as home service, meaning the provider comes to you.
